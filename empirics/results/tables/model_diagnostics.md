## M2. Cross-sectional dependence of the residuals (Pesaran CD test)

H0: residuals are uncorrelated across states. Pairs of states with at least 10 common years. With year effects in the model, the average correlation is mechanically close to zero, so the test picks up common shocks that hit states with different strength. A rejection argues for Driscoll–Kraay standard errors or the country bootstrap as a check.

"outcome varies" drops states whose dependent variable is constant (e.g. never in conflict): for them the 
residual is minus the year effects, identical across these states, which creates correlation mechanically.
