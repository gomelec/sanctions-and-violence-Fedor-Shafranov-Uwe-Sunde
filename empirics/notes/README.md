## Headline findings

1. **The data allow a VAR in levels.** All series are stationary; persistence is well below 1 (03). All
   estimated VARs are stable (06).
2. **Violence → sanctions is robust.** Senders impose new sanctions after conflict. This holds in the Granger
   tests without controls (04), the lag grid, the response functions (07) and 11 of 12 IRF versions (08);
   also in the LP-DiD extension (+12.8 pp in the onset year, 12). Same-sample control check (09 part 4): incidence
   significant in every control version; levels lose significance only with GDP growth (a likely bad control, D8).
   Unaffected by the Yugoslavia check (09 part 5).
   **Not reproduced with GDELT** (11): insignificant in all OLS versions; but the GDELT incidence measure tracks
   UCDP conflict poorly, so this is not decisive.
3. **Sanctions → violence: fewer deaths two years later; no robust effect on conflict incidence.**
   (Revised 2026-10-23 after 09 parts 4–5, master run 16:50.)
   - *Levels:* log deaths per 100k are lower two years after a new case: sum of the cross-lags −0.035 with FE only
     (p = 0.028) to −0.050 with all controls (p = 0.018), significant in every control version on a common sample
     (09 part 4), with the jackknife and GMM (04, 10), and in PPML on winsorised deaths (−11% FE only, −16% with
     controls, both p = 0.002; 09). Capped raw deaths: Granger p = 0.036 with controls, < 0.1 without (09).
     Weaker without the ex-Yugoslav states (FE only −0.024, p = 0.080; 09 part 5). The cumulative IRF (no same-year
     effect) is negative but not significant: −0.107 [−0.256, 0.061] FE only, −0.150 [−0.300, 0.006] with controls.
   - *Incidence:* the earlier −3.5 pp at t−2 (p = 0.026) requires the sanction-stock control in t−1, which
     mechanically contains the tested lags; with FE only, the other controls, or the stock dated t−3 the sum is
     ≈ 0 (09 part 4). The same holds for the logit APE (−0.046) and the significant incidence IRF (−0.077,
     08). Not a finding.
   - *Caveats:* if sanctions may affect violence within the year, the IRF turns positive (08); violence peaks in
     the sanction year (07; also LP-DiD, 12). GDELT (11): incidence insignificant; media-reported violence in
     levels *rises* after sanctions (+0.027, p = 0.027).
4. **Sanctions coincide with escalations.** Violence is elevated before, and peaks in, the sanction year (07).
   Annual data cannot separate who moved first within the year.
5. **Overall:** senders react to violence (UCDP); sanctions are followed by somewhat fewer deaths two years later,
   but no robust change in whether a country is in conflict, and possibly more low-level violence (GDELT). Not a
   symmetric cycle of violence; the sanction effect is small, partly driven by the Yugoslav cases, and depends on
   the within-year timing assumption.

## Open diagnostic points

- Small residual autocorrelation at lag 2 in the sender equation (06).
- Weak cross-sectional dependence (mean residual correlation 0.01–0.03): Driscoll–Kraay check in 09.
- GMM incidence models: Hansen J rejects or is borderline (10).
